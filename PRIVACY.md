FullDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Introduction

FullDex is an independent, open-source gaming application that allows users to play supported games using their own legally obtained game files.

This Privacy Policy explains how FullDex handles information when users access its features, including local game saves, optional Google Drive cloud saves, music playback, RetroAchievements and bug reporting.

FullDex is developed and maintained as an independent, community-driven project.

Official repository: https://github.com/mamesj/pokemon-fulldex

2. Local Game Data

FullDex stores certain information locally on your device to provide its core functionality. This may include:

- Game save files and save states.
- Application settings and preferences.
- Game library information and metadata.
- User-provided ROM files and artwork.
- Diagnostic logs.
- Music player preferences and configured media sources.

Locally stored information remains on your device unless you choose to use a feature that transfers it elsewhere, such as cloud saving or submitting a bug report.

FullDex does not require a Google account for local gameplay.

3. Google Drive Cloud Saves

FullDex provides an optional Google Drive integration that allows users to back up, restore and, where supported, synchronise game save files.

When you connect your Google account, FullDex requests the Google Drive permissions displayed during the OAuth authorisation process.

These permissions are used to provide cloud-save functionality, including:

- Uploading game save files to Google Drive.
- Downloading previously backed-up game saves.
- Managing cloud-save files as necessary for backup and restoration.
- Synchronising supported game progress between compatible devices.

FullDex uses Google's OAuth authorisation process and does not request your Google account password.

Google Drive integration is optional. Users can play games and maintain local saves without connecting a Google account.

FullDex does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

Google user data accessed through this integration is intended solely to provide and maintain the user-requested cloud-save functionality.

FullDex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

The exact permissions requested, authentication-token storage and cloud-save data flow must match the released application's implementation.

4. Google Account Access and Revocation

You can disconnect FullDex from your Google account at any time by visiting:

https://myaccount.google.com/connections

Revoking access prevents FullDex from performing further authorised Google Drive operations unless you reconnect.

Revoking access does not automatically delete cloud-save files already stored in Google Drive.

Users can manage and delete their cloud-save files through the applicable Google Drive storage controls.

5. Music Player and Media Sources

FullDex includes an optional music player that allows users to play audio from supported media sources.

Depending on the source selected, FullDex may access:

- Local audio files selected by the user.
- Audio streams or files retrieved from external media URLs.
- Media source information necessary to provide playback.
- Playback settings and preferences stored locally.

When users play audio from an external media source, the provider may receive information necessary to deliver the content, such as network requests, IP addresses and requested media URLs.

External providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

FullDex does not sell users' music libraries or listening information.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights or permissions to access and play their selected media.

The music player is optional and does not require Google Drive cloud-save access.

6. RetroAchievements

FullDex may integrate with RetroAchievements to provide optional achievement tracking and related gameplay features.

When users enable this integration, relevant account and gameplay information may be exchanged with RetroAchievements to authenticate users and provide achievement functionality.

RetroAchievements is an independent third-party service and operates according to its own privacy practices.

Website: https://retroachievements.org

7. Bug Reports and Diagnostic Information

Users may submit bug reports through GitHub Issues or supported in-app reporting features.

Depending on the reporting method and information supplied, reports may contain:

- Application version and operating system.
- Device information.
- Error messages and diagnostic logs.
- Screenshots.
- Optional game save files.
- User-provided descriptions of technical problems.

Users should review diagnostic files and attachments before submitting them, as these may contain technical or personal information.

Reports submitted through public GitHub Issues may be publicly visible.

Any separate private diagnostic-upload service will be described in this policy when implemented.

8. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

FullDex does not sell personal information.

When users enable optional integrations, information necessary to provide those features may be processed by third-party services, including:

- Google Drive: Cloud-save storage and retrieval.
- RetroAchievements: Achievement tracking and account-related functionality.
- External media providers: Delivery of user-selected audio.
- GitHub: Bug reports, project discussions and support requests.

These services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms.

FullDex does not use Google user data for targeted advertising.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data remains on your device until deleted by you or removed through applicable application or operating-system processes.

Google Drive cloud-save files remain subject to the storage and deletion controls available for the relevant Google account.

Revoking Google account access does not necessarily delete previously uploaded files.

Bug reports and information submitted through GitHub are subject to GitHub's retention and deletion practices.

Users may request assistance with privacy-related questions or deletion procedures through the project's support channels.

10. Children's Privacy

FullDex is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Optional third-party services may impose their own age restrictions or parental consent requirements.

If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact the project maintainer.

11. Security

FullDex aims to follow appropriate security practices to protect information handled by the application.

Google account authorisation is performed using Google's OAuth system.

Users are encouraged to maintain independent backups of important game save files.

No application, network transmission or online storage system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to FullDex features, integrations or data-handling practices.

Updates will be published in the official GitHub repository with a revised date.
